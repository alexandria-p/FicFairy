# FicFairy Privacy Policy

_This is a web-page copy of [FicFairy_Privacy-Policy.docx](FicFairy_Privacy-Policy.docx). Both say the same thing._

## 1. Introduction

FicFairy are referred to in this Privacy Policy as we, us, our, or this app.

This Privacy Policy explains how we manage personal information. It also describes your rights to access and correct personal information we hold about you, and how you can make a complaint about our management of your personal information.

### 1.1 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.

The current version will be available on our website <https://github.com/alexandria-p/ficfairy>.

## 2. Personal information: what we collect

### 2.1 Types of personal information

We do not collect any details or personal information. This has been a deliberate choice in this app’s development to keep it minimal, ethical and secure.

This app is downloaded to your personal device and, from there, makes any outgoing calls to integrated services (Dropbox, Archive of Our Own) using a HTTPS call directly from your machine to the 3rd party. There is no intermediary server or API maintained by FicFairy that this traffic passes through. FicFairy does not run any servers or databases, and does not collect data from your usage or device.

Some changes the user makes to their App Settings (e.g. timezone, appearance) are saved to your local browser storage. It can be removed by clicking ‘Reset to defaults’.

If the user wishes to report a bug or feedback, they may optionally attach their name and a log of their app usage to assist with troubleshooting.

All feedback or bug submissions are publicly listed at <https://github.com/alexandria-p/ficfairy/issues/>

Logs include:

- The browser you are using (e.g. Chrome/156.0.0.0 Safari/537.36)
- The device you are using (e.g. Macintosh; Intel Mac OS X 10_15_7)
- What run you decided to perform
- The run logs (including the id and names of the works, if any were retrieved)

PII (Personally identifiable information) such as your AO3 username, AO3 password, timezone and your IP address are never saved to, or included in, the logs.

When the Feedback form is opened in the app, Cloudflare ensures you are a real human and not a bot and helps send your feedback to GitHub. Your IP address and country are logged to Cloudflare as part of this bot-mitigation, and will be deleted after 30 days.

## 3. How we collect personal information

### 3.1 Sources of information

As per 2.1, we do not have a server or database to store or collect personal information.

Any personal information voluntarily submitted via the feedback service is publicly listed at <https://github.com/alexandria-p/ficfairy/issues/>, and is removable upon request.

### 3.2 Anonymity and Pseudonymity

You may use FicFairy entirely anonymously if you wish, by simply downloading the app and using it on your device. Information is only submitted once you open and submit a Feedback form in-app.

## 4. Why we collect personal information and how we use it

We collect, hold, use and disclose information in Cloudflare and GitHub for the primary purpose of providing technical support to our users, complying with regulatory obligations.

## 5. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not disclose personal information to third parties. You may opt-into third-party services such as Dropbox to save FicFairy files, indexing and logging to your personal Dropbox account.

## 6. Security of Personal Information

### 6.1 Security Measures

We do not hold your personal information on any servers or databases. Information submitted via Feedback form passes through Cloudflare and is publicly listed on GitHub, and is therefore stored on GitHub’s servers. The request log (IP address and country of origin), but not the details of the request, are stored on Cloudflare’s servers for 30 days. It is our practice to require our storage service providers to be reputable.

### 6.2 Retention and Destruction

Feedback will be de-listed and deleted or removed from Github Issues upon request. Cloudflare logs automatically are deleted after 30 days, but can be individually removed upon request if you provide your IP address and country of origin.

## 7. Third party websites

Our website may contain links to third party websites, such as the Dropbox data storage service. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

## 8. Access and Correction

### 8.1 Access to and correction of your Personal Information

You have the right to request access to personal information. To make an access request, please contact us, and we’ll help you find your information and facilitate its removal.

## 9. Complaints and questions

If you have a question about this policy or complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us. We aim to resolve complaints within 30 days. If we need more time, we will keep you informed of our progress.
