Before you choose a folder

A downloaded file is paired with its AO3 work by the work ID at the start of its file name, followed by a space, _, . or -. Anything after that is free.

The ideal name ends with the date AO3 last updated the work, as YYYY-MM-DD, after a space and just before the extension - it is how a later run tells your copy is behind AO3's:

1234567&ngsp;The Trees Are Burning - library_of_alexandria&ngsp;2024-12-14.html work ID · title - author · date updated

✓ 1234567 The Trees Are Burning - library_of_alexandria 2024-12-14.html
✓ 1234567 The Trees Are Burning - library_of_alexandria.html — matched, but with no date: you are asked what to do about it
× The Trees Are Burning 1234567.html — the number is not first
× 1234567The Trees Are Burning.html — nothing separates the number from the title

Files named any other way still load, and nothing will look broken - but they cannot be matched to your index, so every title opens on AO3 instead of your own copy. Every file a run downloads is named the ideal way already.

The folder you choose becomes your library. If it does not already have them, it gets five folders of its own - indexing, collections, images, runs and works - and downloaded works live in works.
